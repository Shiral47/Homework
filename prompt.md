Dana's Bakery Order System - prompts:

1. The first Prompt Given to Codex
Please create a system that processes orders for Dana's Bakery in Nachlaot. The system gets orders with four pieces of information: the product, quantity, day of the week, and customer city. If the city is left blank, the customer is picking up from the shop. Our menu consists of challah for 18₪, babka for 45₪, a box of rugelach for 30₪, and sourdough loaf for 25₪. Croissants (12₪) are on our old website menu, but we stopped making them, so they cannot be ordered anymore. Today's inventory starts with 10 challahs, 4 sourdough loaves, 3 boxes of rugelach, and 0 babkas.
If an item is out of stock, suggest a replacement product following this exact sequence: if there is no babka, offer rugelach; if no rugelach, offer sourdough; if no sourdough, offer challah. A replacement only counts if we have enough inventory to cover the full requested quantity. The customer keeps their original quantity and pays the price of the new item. However, only suggest up to two times—if the second suggestion doesn't work either, apologize and cancel the order. If the form sends invalid entries like zero or negative quantities, or items like "pizza", politely inform the customer that the order could not be understood.
For delivery, shipping inside Jerusalem costs 20₪. We do not deliver outside Jerusalem, so if an order is placed for another city, keep the order, switch it to free pickup, and inform the customer. Orders over 150₪ get a 15% discount on the baked goods, but this discount never applies on Fridays, and it never applies to the delivery fee.
Finally, write customer responses in a warm, personal tone as if Dana wrote them. For every processed order, keep a step-by-step diary log of all the stages the order went through.
